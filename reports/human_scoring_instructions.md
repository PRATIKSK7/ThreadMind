# Human Scoring Instructions for ThreadMind Replier

> **Status**: The `human_*_1_to_5` columns in `reports/human_scoring_subset.csv` currently contain scores generated by an automated Independent LLM Scorer (Llama 3.2), NOT by a human annotator. To complete genuine human validation, a human must independently re-score the 30 examples using this rubric and overwrite those columns.

This document outlines the rubric for manually scoring the 30 generated replies in `reports/human_scoring_subset.csv`.

## Goal
Evaluate the AI agent's generated reply across 4 specific dimensions, scoring each from **1 to 5** (1 = Completely Unacceptable, 5 = Excellent).

## Instructions
1. Open `reports/human_scoring_subset.csv` (which will be generated upon completion of the pipeline).
2. For each row, read the `conversation_history` to understand the customer's issue.
3. Read the `agent_reply`.
4. Provide an integer score (1-5) for the following columns:

### Dimension 1: Correctness (`human_correctness_1_to_5`)
- **5 (Excellent)**: Directly addresses the customer's actual issue and is completely relevant to the latest message.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Addresses the issue but misses minor nuances or provides a generic but correct response.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Completely misses the point, answers the wrong question, or provides wildly incorrect information.

### Dimension 2: Groundedness (`human_groundedness_1_to_5`)
- **5 (Excellent)**: Completely grounded. The agent does NOT invent tracking numbers, order IDs, refunds, or internal policies.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Mostly grounded, but might assume a generic policy (e.g., "Returns take 3-5 days") without explicit context.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Blatantly invents false facts (e.g., "Your package is currently in Seattle" when the customer never provided a tracking number).

### Dimension 3: Helpfulness (`human_helpfulness_1_to_5`)
- **5 (Excellent)**: Pushes the conversation toward resolution. If info is missing, it explicitly asks for it. If escalation is needed, it politely informs the customer.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Provides some help but could be more proactive in resolving the issue.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Unhelpful, confusing, or creates more work for the customer without a clear path forward.

### Dimension 4: Tone (`human_tone_1_to_5`)
- **5 (Excellent)**: Empathetic, polite, and professional. Natural conversational flow.
- **3 (Acceptable)**: Professional but slightly robotic, repetitive, or templated.
- **1 (Unacceptable)**: Rude, dismissive, sarcastic, or completely unreadable formatting.

---
**Note:** Once a human annotator independently scores these 30 rows, **Cohen's Kappa** can be calculated to measure agreement between human judgement and the automated LLM Judge. The current scores in the CSV are from an automated Independent LLM Scorer and do not constitute human evaluation.
